Our purpose

Education rooted in values, designed for possibility.

We help every learner build the knowledge, skills, discipline, and character to become a capable and responsible citizen.

Founder & Chairman

Shailendra Singh

The vision of this institution was first dreamt by my respected father, Late Shri Shiv Moul Singh. Established in 2000, the school remains devoted to quality education that empowers students from our rural surroundings.

I am proud to see our alumni making valuable contributions across the country. We will continue to nurture character and inspire every student to dream big and reach their fullest potential.

Their achievements are a reflection of the dedication and values that this institution strives to instil in every student.

I firmly believe that the students who graduate from this institution in the years to come will continue this legacy of excellence. They will bring pride and honour to their parents, society, and the nation, and will contribute meaningfully towards the progress and development of our country. Our commitment remains unwavering to provide quality education, nurture character, and inspire every student to dream big and achieve their fullest potential.

Principal

Rajesh Pratap Singh

Dear Students and Parents, Warm greetings!

Our school, Shivmoul Singh KP Singh Memorial Inter College, Lachchipur, Semari – Raebareli, is a a centre of learning and values.

Our aim is not only to impart academic knowledge but also to develop strong moral values, discipline, and patriotism in our students. Situated in a rural area, our college provides modern educational facilities, including qualified teachers, science laboratories, computer education, and sports, for the all-round development of every student.

We believe that the success of our students is the true success of our institution. We encourage every student to work hard, study with dedication, achieve their goals, and make their parents and teachers proud.

The school is always with you.
